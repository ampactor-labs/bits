# Device smoke checklist

Run on both phones after each push. All of it is one flow: make a real bit.

1. Open ampactor.dev/bits in Chrome; install to home screen; launch standalone.
2. New bit: mic permission prompts once; record you two doing a 15s bit; done.
3. Beat ticks appear on the progress bar where the track actually hits.
4. Photo puppet: a person photo cuts out clean; a random object falls back to
   the whole frame instead of failing.
5. Doodle: finger drawing keeps; the lines visibly boil.
6. Backdrop: a camera-roll photo fills the stage behind everything.
7. Drag a puppet to place it; two fingers resize and rotate it; reopen the bit
   and the staging survived.
8. Long-press a puppet: it drops from the cast; undo brings it back.
9. Snip: draw a line across a puppet; the far side falls into a dangling piece
   that swings when you shake the puppet.
10. Mouth: tap one onto a face; it flaps in time with the recorded voices,
    shut during silence.
11. Record a pass: audio plays, dragging feels springy (lag, lean, squash).
12. Overdub: a second pass records while the first replays; both survive.
13. Undo removes only the last move.
14. Render completes with progress; the export matches the preview exactly:
    motion, dangles, mouth timing, boil.
15. Share hands the mp4 to a messaging app; voices are in sync.
16. Airplane mode, relaunch: everything above still works offline.

Record failures with phone model + Chrome version in an issue.
